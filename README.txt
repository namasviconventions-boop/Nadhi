NADHI WINDOORS WEBSITE
======================

Files:
- index.html: responsive company website.

Preview:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge, or another browser.
3. Internet access is needed to load the sample photos.

Before publishing:
- Replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com in index.html with your real business email address.
- Replace the contact placeholders with your company phone number and WhatsApp number.
- Replace the sample photos with your own product, factory, team, and completed-project photos.
- The quote form currently opens an email draft; it does not store submissions online.
- For a live public website, upload the files to a hosting service and connect your domain name.
